Critical exam trap: RAID is not backup

RAID primarily supports availability and fault tolerance against drive failure. Backups preserve recoverable historical copies. RAID alone does not protect you from ransomware encryption, accidental deletion, application corruption, catastrophic chassis loss, or every site-level event.
